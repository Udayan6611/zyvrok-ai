## Brand & Style
The design system is built for a micro-SaaS environment that prioritizes speed of execution and clarity of data. The brand personality is "Technically Sophisticated yet Radically Simple." It avoids the coldness of traditional enterprise software by utilizing soft shadows and intentional color accents, creating an interface that feels like a premium productivity tool rather than a utility.

The aesthetic follows a **Modern Corporate** style with **Minimalist** influences. It utilizes high-quality whitespace to reduce cognitive load during complex AI-driven workflows. Visual interest is maintained through subtle "glass" overlays on elevated surfaces and precise, high-contrast interactive elements.

## Colors
The palette is centered around **Viral Purple (#8B5CF6 / #6B38D4)**, used exclusively for primary actions, success states, and brand-defining accents. This is contrasted against a deep **Slate/Indigo (#0F172A / #0B1C30)** for high-contrast typography and navigation elements.
