02 · Privacy Policy

Data Security & Storage

We respect user privacy. We store user account email addresses and generation counts strictly for authentication and credit tracking in Supabase PostgreSQL (secured with Row Level Security). Payment transactions are processed directly by 256-bit encrypted Razorpay infrastructure; credit card numbers, CVVs, and banking credentials are never handled or stored on our servers.

Zyvrok credits (Starter Pack ₹199 for 50 credits, Creator Pro ₹499 for 150 credits) are digital goods delivered immediately to your account ledger upon transaction confirmation.
